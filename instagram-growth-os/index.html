GROWTHOS

Before the company can operate, the CEO needs three answers.

1 · What is your niche?

Pick the closest preset, or type your own. Everything downstream — offers, hooks, pricing, calendar — is derived from this.

2 · What is your current follower count?

Be honest. The whole plan changes at 1K, 10K and 50K — those are the real business milestones.

3 · What is the goal — followers or money?

4 · Company capacity

A business plan that ignores your real capacity is a fantasy. This sizes the whole operation.
